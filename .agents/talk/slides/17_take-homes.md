# Slide 17 — Take-homes

1. TITLE
Take-homes

2. MAIN CONCEPT
- **Head (the juice):** one sparsified diffusion operator, one null (matched-strength), three results.
  The message is **information-DEPTH, not exclusive detection**. Simple pairwise FC is a **robust,
  complementary detector** — a scalar verdict per band ("did the reorganization lean back toward the
  task?"), and several bands say yes. The multiscale read is a **structured object** — a hierarchy per
  phase, a ρ_sym(τ) curve per band — that adds three things: **which bands** (discrimination: only α/β),
  **at what scale** (β scale-invariant vs α single-scale), and a **higher-order** coarse-scale view the
  edges miss. That is the value-add.
- **FRAME (take-home 1):** raw FC detects broadly and robustly; the multiscale hierarchy *discriminates*
  (α/β), *grades scale* (only β is genuinely multiscale), and adds a *higher-order* lens — complementary
  to the edges, not a replacement. Matched-strength is the sole null, read per-scale.
- **RESULT 1 (take-home 2) — a held, genuinely multiscale trace of reasoning.** β scale-invariant
  (16/16 scales, p .014→.001), α single-scale/mesoscale (12/16, .024→.007). **Held, not replayed**
  (sustained, reinstatement 10/10). Its home is **coarse (hemisphere-level), not fine**: β enriches in
  the **left hemisphere** and is a **cognitive** trace — it *spares* the seizure zone. α is placeless.
- **RESULT 2 (take-home 3) — offline abstraction.** Rest keeps not just the pairs the brain **saw**
  (encoding) but the order it **inferred** (inference) — and inference is **α/β, not β-only** (β 7/16 .005,
  α 7/16 .024). It is **mesoscale-emergent** — a re-grouping visible only when you zoom out, the shape of
  an abstraction.
- **RESULT 3 + BAND-DISSOCIATION (take-home 4) — one operator, two readouts.** The **same** diffusion
  operator marks epileptogenic tissue (δ/low-γ/β; strongest δ, AUC .83). The bands dissociate cleanly —
  **cognition = α/β · epilepsy = δ/low-γ/β · β = the bridge** — and the *one* focal anatomical address in
  the whole talk is the **disease** one, low-γ→SOZ.
- **WHY IT MATTERS:** one framework reads the three axes of the multiscale brain in a single lens — time
  (frequency bands), space (high-resolution sEEG), topology (the diffusion hierarchy) — a reusable,
  null-validated signature detector, provided every claim is discounted against the boring explanation
  (matched-strength: where connectivity is simply strong).

4. SPEECH
Four things to take home. First, the frame. Simple pairwise coherence gives you a scalar per band — did
the reorganization lean back toward the task — and it's a robust detector; several bands say yes. The
multiscale diffusion read gives you a structured object instead, and that buys three things a scalar
can't: which bands carry it — only alpha and beta; at what scale — only beta is genuinely multiscale; and
a higher-order, coarse-scale view the edges miss. It complements the edges, it doesn't replace them.
Second: reasoning leaves a held trace — sustained, not replayed — and it has a coarse, hemisphere-level
home, the left hemisphere, on healthy cortex, sparing the seizure zone. Third: what persists isn't just the pairs the
brain saw, it's the order it inferred — in alpha and beta — and it's mesoscale-emergent, a re-grouping you
only see when you zoom out, the shape of an abstraction. Fourth: the same operator, read inside one
recording, marks epileptogenic tissue, and the bands dissociate cleanly — cognition in alpha and beta,
epilepsy in delta, low-gamma and beta, with beta the bridge; and the one sharp anatomical address in the
whole talk belongs to the disease. One lens over time, space and topology, one null throughout.

Careful: NO β→OFC — β has no FINE home; its home is COARSE (left hemisphere), cognitive, SOZ-independent
(NOT "delocalized/placeless" — that was superseded; α is the placeless one). Do NOT say "only multiscale
sees it" / "invisible to simple methods" — raw FC detects β (p=.024); the edge is discrimination +
scale + higher-order, and raw is robust + complementary, never fragile. Inference is α/β, NOT β-only (δ
inference CUT). Matched-strength is the SOLE null — never "drift", "second null", or "survives both
nulls". Never rank bands by the ×-null ratio. N3 scope = SOZ labels, not surgical outcome (triage, not
diagnostic). No behavioural data — "inference / abstraction" is licensed by design + mesoscale-emergence +
literature, never a brain–behaviour correlation. State localization PER BAND (β coarse-left · α placeless
· low-γ→SOZ), never "localization is dead / all delocalized".
