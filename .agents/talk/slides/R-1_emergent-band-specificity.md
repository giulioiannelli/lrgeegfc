# R-1 · The reveal — band-specificity is *emergent*

## Slide placeholder (copy into Canva)

**Main point.** At the **raw edge** level the cross-phase drift is positive in
*every* band (θ ≈ β) and raw β doesn't even clear the null. Read through the
**diffusion hierarchy**, the non-tracing bands **collapse** and **β is retained
and now clears**. Band-specificity is not in the raw data — it **emerges** from
the multiscale read, which earns its keep right here.

**Concepts to land.**
- **Raw edge-level drift is band-blind — and explained by strength:** positive in
  *every* band (**θ +0.12 ≈ β**), and **raw β fails the matched-strength null
  (p = 0.053)** — strength changes reproduce it. No band-specificity, and no trace
  beyond strength, at the raw level.
- **Through the diffusion hierarchy the non-tracers collapse** (θ +0.12 → **−0.04**)
  while **β is retained and now clears the matched-strength null (p = 0.032)**.
  Band-specificity **emerges** from the multiscale read.
- This is where the **multiscale read earns its keep** — the step that turns a
  band-blind raw drift into a band-specific trace.
- **Filtering, not inflation:** the hierarchy **compresses** magnitude (it
  *separates*, it doesn't *amplify*) — so it is *removing* the non-specific drift,
  not boosting β.

## Keep honest (content constraints, not styling)

- **The hierarchy compresses magnitude — it separates, it does NOT amplify.** Say
  this out loud: the reveal is *filtering out* non-specific drift, not inflating β.
- Numbers are **ρ_sym**: raw θ +0.12 · raw β p = 0.053 (**fails**); multiscale
  θ −0.04 · β p = 0.032 (**clears**).
- **Don't overclaim β's size** — multiscale β is still +0.20 (read it as 16.6× the
  null, per M-4). The point is *specificity emerges*, not that β becomes large.
- Everything here is **matched-strength gated** (M-4); raw drift alone ≠ trace.
- **The raw trace is band-blind and explained by *strength*, not drift.** Raw β
  *clears* the drift null but *fails* matched-strength (p = 0.053); strength changes
  reproduce it. Don't frame the raw trace as drift — the old drift figures are
  retired. The multiscale read is what lifts β past matched-strength (p = 0.032).
