# Slide 6 — Why this matters: signatures across scales

1. TITLE
Why this matters — signatures across scales

2. MAIN CONCEPT
- The stakes are not the method — they are what multiscale structure lets you *see*.
- The phenomena we most want to catch are intrinsically cross-scale: on the cognitive side memory, cognitive maps, consolidation; on the clinical side epileptogenic networks. They live in the interplay of rhythms, regions and relations — exactly what a single-scale metric averages away.
- So multiscale structure is not just more detail: it is a new observable. Read it, pin it to a rhythm, and localize it, and a connectivity matrix becomes a *signature detector* — did this process leave a cross-scale fingerprint, and where?
- Non-negotiable: a signature is only real if it beats the nulls that discount the boring explanations — connection strength and the passage of time. "Multiscale" without that control is just a story.

4. SPEECH
Why should you care beyond the machinery? Because the things we most want to catch — on the cognitive side memory, cognitive maps, consolidation; on the clinical side seizure networks — are intrinsically cross-scale. They live in the interplay of rhythms, regions and relations, which is precisely what a single-scale metric averages away. So multiscale structure isn't just extra detail — it's a new kind of observable. Read that structure, pin it to a rhythm, and localize it, and you've turned a connectivity matrix into a signature detector: did this process leave a cross-scale fingerprint — and where? That's the promise. And here's the catch I'll keep repeating for the rest of the talk: a multiscale signature only counts if it beats the boring explanations — that the change was just connection strength, or just the passage of time. Strip those out first, or it's a nice story, not a result.

Careful: this slide sells the *promise* — a signature detector — so keep it motivational; the only validated signature in this talk is the β cophenetic trace (results start slide 12), so don't imply we have already detected consolidation or seizure fingerprints ourselves.
