# Slide 16 — β → OFC: a held consolidation

1. TITLE
β → orbitofrontal cortex — held, not replayed

2. MAIN CONCEPT
- WHERE. The β cophenetic trace concentrates in the orbitofrontal cortex (bilateral, system scale) — matched-strength BH q = 0.009–0.013, across all four conditions, robust to leave-one-out and shaft removal. OFC is the cognitive-map hub (Behrens / Wilson / Schuck) — exactly where a learned relational map should consolidate. Hotspot, not container (~59% within-OFC pro, 5/10 implanted there): concentration above baseline, not "the trace lives only in OFC".
- HOW. It is held, not replayed: sustained across the full ~10 min of post-task rest — a persistent consolidation, not a transient flicker. Reinstatement is cohort-wide, 10/10, p = 0.001 (LOO 0.002); transient/burst replay is a clean negative — that question is closed. And the persistence is a STATE, not an event: where a patient traces in more than one band, the reinstatement holds across all of them at once, not as isolated band-specific replay bursts.
- WHO. The carriers are the LEFT-implanted patients — a stable trait (test–retest ρ = 0.95), not random dropout. (The two resetters are right-hemisphere-only implants; α is a symmetric control.) The variability is signal.

4. SPEECH
So where does the β trace live, and what kind of thing is it? Where: the orbitofrontal cortex — bilaterally, at the system scale, surviving matched-strength with corrected q-values around one percent, across every condition, leave-one-out and shaft-removal included. That's the payoff of the bet — OFC is the classic cognitive-map hub, exactly where a learned relational map should consolidate. One honesty note: it's a hotspot, not a container — concentration above chance, not a claim it lives only there.
And what kind of thing is it? Not a replay blip. The trace is held across the full ten minutes of rest after the task — a sustained reinstatement, ten out of ten patients, one in a thousand — while transient, bursty replay comes back a clean negative. So it's consolidation, not an echo. And the persistence is a genuine state: for a patient who traces, it holds across every band that traces, all at once — not as little replay bursts in one band or another. Finally, why not every patient equally? Because the carriers are the left-implanted patients, and that split is a stable trait — a test–retest correlation of point-nine-five — not random dropout. The variability is signal.

Careful: say "held / sustained / persistent", never "tighter" (tighter is only a trend). Replay = clean negative (transient/burst); the SUSTAINED reinstatement is the positive. OFC = hotspot, not container. The "persistent across every traced band" line is the held-not-replayed result generalized — it is the sustained-vs-transient characterization of the state, NOT a separate gated cohort test; if pressed for the per-patient × per-band decomposition, present it that way, don't assert a new statistic.
