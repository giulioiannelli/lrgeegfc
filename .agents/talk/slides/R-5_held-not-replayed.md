# R-5 · Held, not replayed — and who carries it

## Slide placeholder (copy into Canva)

**Main point.** Post-task rest doesn't *replay* the task in transient bursts — it
**dwells** in the task configuration: a **held state**. Reinstatement is
cohort-unanimous (**10/10, p = 0.001**), at every scale, while transient/replay is a
clean negative down to the ripple floor. And the cohort splits into a **stable
structure** — carriers vs resets — whose driver is **laterality**, not noise.

**Concepts to land.**
- **Held, not replayed.** Post-task rest is *more task-like than pre-task rest* —
  **10/10 patients, p = 0.001, LOO 0.002**, at **every scale**. The trace is a
  **sustained reinstatement**, a dwell in the task configuration — **not** a train of
  transient replay events (transient/burst reactivation is a **clean negative** down
  to the ~0.2 s ripple floor). This is a *held* state.
- **The cohort is structured, not scattered — 3 tiers.** **6 stable tracers**
  (Pat_08 +0.54 … Pat_07 +0.13) · **2 undetermined** (Pat_13, Pat_15) · **2 resets**
  (Pat_10 −0.14, Pat_14). The spread is **the result**, not measurement noise.
- **Who carries it — laterality.** The per-patient spread is a **stable trait**
  (test–retest-like ρ ≈ 0.95, audit_148), and its driver is **implant/trace
  laterality**: carriers are **left-lateralised** (β net **L +0.34 vs R +0.05**),
  and the non-carriers (the resets/undetermined, e.g. Pat_10, Pat_15) are
  **right-only / right-dominant** implants — they can't sample the left-lateralised
  trace. **α is a symmetric control** (no left bias). So the variance has a *reason*.

## Keep honest (content constraints, not styling)

- **Say "held," never "tighter."** Reinstatement (post > pre task-likeness) is the
  strong, unanimous result (10/10, p = 0.001). Post-rest becoming *internally tighter*
  than pre-rest is only a **trend** — don't state it as a finding.
- **Replay is a clean NEGATIVE — the N4 thread is closed.** Transient / burst
  reactivation was tested and does **not** carry the trace; the signal is the
  **sustained** dwell. Never let "held" slide into "we see replay."
- **Pat_15 is UNDETERMINED, not a clean β-anti dropout** (softened 2026-07-04). The
  only biology-driven cohort-anti exception at β LRG is retired to *undetermined*.
- **The laterality is IMPLANT / trace laterality — NOT an OFC-laterality claim.** It
  explains *who carries the trace* (left-lateralised sampling), not *where in OFC* it
  sits (that's R-3). Don't fuse the two.
- **Per-patient fluctuations ARE part of the result** — show the spread (tiers /
  slopes), not one cohort number. The variance is a **stable trait with a driver**
  (laterality, ρ ≈ 0.95), not a nuisance to average away.
- **No behavioural correlation** — "consolidation / held state" is licensed by task
  structure + anatomy + literature, never by performance.
