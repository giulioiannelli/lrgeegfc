# I-6 · Form mirrors form → the bet, stated precisely

> Merged slide (was I-6 "form mirrors form" + I-7 "the bet"). One beat closes
> the intro: the poetic reflection sharpens into the precise hypothesis, then
> hands into Methods. The funnel is **poetic → precise → "can we even measure it?"**

## Slide placeholder (copy into Canva)

**Main point.** Close the intro in one move: a **hierarchical** method (the
dendrogram) reading a **hierarchical** relation (the transitive order) — *form
mirrors form* — sharpened into the exact thing we hunt: a **band-specific, held,
multiscale** trace in post-task rest. Then kick to Methods.

**Concepts to land.**
- **The mirror (closing the I-1 loop).** The relation is a hierarchy (a transitive
  order); our read-out is a hierarchy (the dendrogram). "Form mirrors form" —
  evocative, and it closes back to the I-1 race tree.
  - **Guardrail, said out loud: mirror, not proof.** The dendrogram is hierarchical
    *by construction* — we'd recover a tree for many tasks. We do **not** claim
    cognition is hierarchical *because* our method is. Cash the parallel out **only**
    as the one thing we measure — the β trace — and leave the rest as suggestion.
- **The bet, stated precisely.** What we hunt is a **trace** in `rest_post`:
  reorganized *during* the task **and** still there afterwards — a **joint** event
  (both, not either). It should be **band-specific** (a real effect is
  frequency-selective), **held** (it persists), and **multiscale** (it lives across
  scales, read by the hierarchy).
- **Vocabulary, said once — the four cross-phase phenomena:**
  - **trace** — changed during task **and** stuck (← ours)
  - **anchor** — never changed
  - **reset** — changed **and** reverted
  - **emergent** — new (did not exist before)
- **Hand into Methods:** *"first — can we even measure intracranial connectivity
  without the volume-conduction artifact?"*
