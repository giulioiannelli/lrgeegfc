# Slide 18 — Outlook & thanks

1. TITLE
Outlook & thanks

2. MAIN CONCEPT
- Outlook: bigger and more diverse datasets; other cognitive tasks; a substrate-independent test of the
  multiscale trace; a behavioural link (transitive-inference performance); confirming the coarse-scale
  left-hemisphere home in a larger cohort; a prospective clinical test of the diffusion SOZ marker; and
  settling whether low-γ→SOZ is a task-trace or a stable-tissue anchor.
- The through-line: one multiscale framework, reusable wherever cross-scale signatures are expected.
- Thanks: the Sapienza collaborators (data + task); the LRG authors (Villegas, Gabrielli, Poggialini, Gili).

4. SPEECH
Where next: bigger and more diverse datasets, other tasks, and a substrate-independent version of the
trace; a behavioural link to inference performance; confirming the coarse, left-hemisphere home in a
larger cohort; and a prospective clinical test of the seizure-onset marker — including whether the
low-gamma seizure-zone signal is a genuine task-trace or a stable property of that tissue. All of it rides
the same reusable multiscale lens. Thank you to our Sapienza collaborators for the data and the task, and
to the LRG authors whose framework made this possible.
