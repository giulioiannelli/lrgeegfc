# I-6 · Form mirrors form — a reflection, not a result

## Slide placeholder (copy into Canva)

**Main point.** The poetic through-line, honestly bounded: a **hierarchical**
method (the dendrogram) reading a **hierarchical** relation (the transitive
order) — "a hierarchical neuronal mirror of hierarchical relations." A suggestive
reflection and a closing image — explicitly **not** a proof.

**Concepts to land.**
- The relation is a hierarchy (a transitive order); our read-out is a hierarchy
  (the dendrogram). "Form mirrors form" — evocative, and it closes the loop back
  to the I-1 race tree.
- **Say the guardrail out loud:** this is a **mirror, not proof**. The dendrogram
  is hierarchical **by construction** — we'd recover a tree for many tasks. We do
  **not** claim cognition is hierarchical *because* our method is.
- We cash the parallel out **only** as the one thing we actually measure — the
  **β trace** — and leave the rest as suggestion.
- Use it as an attack line / closing image, never as a load-bearing claim.

**Figures / visuals.**
- Two-panel concept — a **learned-order tree** beside a **connectivity
  dendrogram**, same shape, labelled "suggestive." Build in Canva.

**References.** None required (this is rhetorical framing). Optional nod to a

*Nat. Neurosci.* https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-021-00916-3
