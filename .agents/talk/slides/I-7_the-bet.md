# I-7 · The bet, stated precisely

## Slide placeholder (copy into Canva)

**Main point.** State the hunt in one line before methods: a **band-specific,
held, multiscale** trace in post-task rest — a **joint** event that reorganizes
*during* the task AND survives *into* `rest_post`.

**Concepts to land.**
- What we hunt: a **trace** in `rest_post` — reorganized during the task **and**
  still there afterwards. A **joint** event (both, not either).
- It should be **band-specific** (a real effect is frequency-selective),
  **held** (it persists), and **multiscale** (it lives across scales, read by the
  hierarchy).
- Vocabulary, said once — the four cross-phase phenomena:
  - **trace** — changed during task **and** stuck (← ours)
  - **anchor** — never changed
  - **reset** — changed **and** reverted
  - **emergent** — new (did not exist before)
- Hand into Methods: *"first — can we even measure intracranial connectivity
  without the volume-conduction artifact?"*
