# Slide 21 — Take-homes

1. TITLE
Take-homes

2. MAIN CONCEPT
- Head (the juice): one sparsified diffusion operator, one null (matched-strength), three
  results — and the message is **information-DEPTH, not exclusive detection**. Simple
  pairwise FC gives a *scalar verdict per band* ("did the reorganization lean back toward
  the task?") and several bands say yes. The multiscale read gives a *structured object* — a
  hierarchy per phase, a ρ_sym(τ) curve per band — that characterizes WHICH bands
  (selectivity: only α/β), AT WHAT SCALE (scale-shape: β scale-invariant, α single-scale),
  and THROUGH WHICH PATHWAYS (higher-order walks). That is the value-add, not "seeing what
  simple methods can't."
- FRAME (take-home 1): raw FC is information-poor — a scalar per band; the multiscale
  diffusion hierarchy is a structured object that *grades* the trace by scale-shape and
  anatomy. Only α/β fire (selectivity); of those, only β is genuinely multiscale
  (scale-invariant). Matched-strength is the sole null, read per-scale — never best-scale.
- RESULT 1 (take-home 2): transitive-inference leaves a **held, genuinely multiscale trace**
  — β scale-invariant (16/16 scales, p .014→.001), α single-scale/mesoscale (12/16, .024→.007).
  It is **held, not replayed** (sustained proximity, reinstatement 10/10, p=.001 ⚠️), and
  **delocalized** — no single cortical address at any scale; inter-patient spread tracks
  implant laterality (β trace ∝ left-contact fraction, ρ=+0.685, p=.029).
- RESULT 2 (take-home 3): rest keeps not just the pairs the brain **saw** (encoding) but the
  order it **inferred** (inference) — and inference is δ/α/β, **not β-only** (β 7/16 .005, α
  7/16 .024). Encoding and inference sit in **different cortex** — OFC vs cingulate — a
  weak, whole-graph localization trend where the value-add is *where*, not detection.
- RESULT 3 + BAND-DISSOCIATION (take-home 4): the **same diffusion operator** marks
  epileptogenic tissue (δ/low-γ/β; strongest in δ, AUC .83). The bands dissociate cleanly —
  **cognition = α/β · epilepsy = δ/low-γ/β · β = the bridge** — the one operator spans a
  cognitive signature and a clinical marker.
- WHY IT MATTERS: one framework puts the three axes of the multiscale brain in a single
  lens — time (frequency bands), space (high-resolution sEEG contacts), topology (the
  diffusion hierarchy) — a reusable, null-validated signature detector, provided every
  claim is discounted against the boring explanation (matched-strength: where connectivity
  is simply strong).

4. SPEECH
Four things to take home. First, the frame: simple pairwise coherence gives you a scalar
per band — did the reorganization lean back toward the task — and several bands say yes.
The multiscale diffusion read gives you a structured object instead, a hierarchy per phase,
so it can tell you which bands and at what scale. Only alpha and beta carry the trace, and
of those only beta is genuinely multiscale — present at every scale. Second result: reasoning
leaves a held trace — post-task rest stays close to the task network, sustained, not replayed
in bursts — and it's delocalized, spread across cortex, with the between-patient differences
tracking implant laterality rather than one hotspot. Third: what persists isn't just the pairs
the brain saw, it's the order it inferred, and encoding and inference sit in different cortex —
premises nearer orbitofrontal, inferred order nearer cingulate. Fourth: the same operator, read
inside a single recording, marks epileptogenic tissue — and the bands dissociate cleanly:
cognition in alpha and beta, epilepsy in delta, low-gamma and beta, with beta the bridge between
them. One lens over time, space and topology, and one null throughout — matched-strength.

Careful: no β→OFC — the β trace is DELOCALIZED; OFC is the ENCODING anchor only (result 2),
never the home of the β trace. Do NOT say "only multiscale sees it" / "invisible to simple
methods" / "multiscale-exclusive" — raw FC detects β (p=.024) and Grassmann sees it too; the
edge is selectivity + scale-shape, not exclusive detection. Inference is δ/α/β, NOT β-only, and
the δ inference-specific effect is CUT (partial-correlation artifact). Matched-strength is the
SOLE null — drift is retired; never "drift survivor", "second null", or "survives both nulls".
Never rank bands by the ×-null ratio. Localization / reinstatement / laterality are whole-graph
(⚠️), re-derivation on mst@0.20 pending — present as trends, not addresses. N3 scope = SOZ
labels, not surgical outcome; a triage marker, not a diagnostic. No behavioural data —
"inference / abstraction" is licensed by design + anatomy + literature, never a
brain–behaviour correlation.
