# Slide 19 — The band dissociation: β is the bridge

1. TITLE
The band dissociation — cognition, epilepsy, and the β bridge

2. MAIN CONCEPT
- **Head (the juice):** one operator, read across every band, sorts the frequencies into
  two families — **cognition lives in α/β, epilepsy lives in δ/low-γ/β** — and **β sits in
  both**. That overlap is not a contradiction; β is the *bridge* from the reasoning trace
  into the clinical coda. The bands dissociating cleanly is itself the selling point: it
  shows the multiscale read *characterizes* frequencies (which band, what it carries), it
  isn't a global shift lighting everything up.
- One glance across all bands — don't go band by band, read the dissociation:

  | band | cognitive trace (held reasoning) | epileptogenic marker (SOZ affinity) |
  |------|----------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
  | θ | **silent** (0/16 scales) | — |
  | α | **mesoscale, single-scale** (12/16, p .024→.007, peaks s≈5) | weak (AUC .61) |
  | **β** | **scale-invariant, genuinely multiscale** (16/16, p .014→.001) | **yes (AUC .745)** — the **BRIDGE** |
  | low-γ | trace-null whole-brain (0/16) — **masks a focal cingulate ENCODING trace** (8/8, q .035) ⚠️ | yes (AUC .82) |
  | δ | no cognitive trace (8/16 patchy — not claimed) | **strongest (AUC .83)** |
  | high-γ | not claimed (4/16, coarse-only artifact) | — |

- **Cognition = α · β.** α is the *single-scale* carrier (a mesoscale peak, real but resolvable
  by ordinary measures); β is the *scale-invariant* one, present at every scale — held, and
  delocalized (no single anatomical address).
- **Epilepsy = δ · low-γ · β**, strongest in δ. δ marks the seizure zone but carries **no**
  cognitive trace — the cleanest face of the dissociation.
- **β = the bridge:** the one band that both holds the reasoning trace *and* reads the
  seizure zone. This is the hand-off into slide 20 (the epileptogenic marker).
- **θ-silence is the control.** θ says nothing in either family — and that silence is the
  evidence: a global artefact or a recording shift would light *every* band. Selective
  firing ⇒ band-selectivity is real.
- **Never rank bands by the ×-null ratio.** The gate is the matched-strength cohort test
  read per-scale, not the biggest single-patient number.

4. SPEECH
Zoom out to every band at once — the point isn't a catalogue, it's a dissociation. The
same operator sorts the frequencies into two families, with one band in both. Cognition
lives in alpha and beta: beta carries a held trace that's present at every scale —
genuinely multiscale, sixteen of sixteen — and alpha carries a single mesoscale one, twelve
of sixteen, real but resolvable by ordinary measures. Epilepsy lives in delta, low-gamma,
and beta: delta is the strongest seizure-zone marker, yet it carries no cognitive trace at
all — that's the cleanest face of the split. And beta sits in both families. It's the one
band that both holds the reasoning trace and reads the seizure zone, and that's the bridge
into the clinical result on the next slide. Two more things earn their place on this table.
Theta is silent everywhere — and that silence is the control: if any of this were a global
recording artefact, every band would light up, so selective firing tells us the
band-specificity is real. And low-gamma looks empty whole-brain, but that zero masks a focal
cingulate trace that is specifically encoding — the learning phase — in every patient
sampled. One operator, and the bands sort themselves into cognition and clinic.

Careful: β is the bridge because it appears in BOTH families — do NOT say β localizes to
OFC (β is delocalized; OFC is the encoding anchor, slide 18 only). Do NOT say "only the
multiscale read sees these bands" (raw FC detects β too — the edge is selectivity + scale-
shape, not exclusive detection). Do NOT rank bands by the ×-null ratio. Do NOT invoke drift
(retired — matched-strength is the sole null; read per-scale, never best-scale). The
low-γ→cingulate encoding number is whole-graph (⚠️), re-derivation pending — say it as a
focal encoding trace, not a settled localization. β appearing in both sets is coherent
(the bridge), not a contradiction.
