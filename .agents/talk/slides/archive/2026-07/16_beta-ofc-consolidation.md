# Slide 16 — The β trace: held, placeless, lateralized

1. TITLE
The β trace — held, placeless, carried by the left implant

2. MAIN CONCEPT
- HEAD (the juice). Having graded the β trace as the genuinely-multiscale carrier, we now ask *what kind of thing it is*. Three answers: it is **held** (a sustained state across the whole post-task rest, not a replay burst), it is **placeless** (no single anatomical home at any scale — this overturns our earlier "β → OFC" claim), and its patient-to-patient strength is set by **implant laterality** (the left-hemisphere carriers hold the trace; the right-only implants reset).
- HELD, not replayed. The β proximity is sustained across the full ~10 min of rest after the task — cohort-wide reinstatement 10/10, p = 0.001 (leave-one-out 0.002). Transient / burst replay is a clean negative — that question is closed. It is a persistent STATE, not an event: where a patient traces in more than one band, the reinstatement holds across all of them at once, not as isolated band-specific bursts. (⚠️ whole-graph number; re-derivation on mst@0.20 pending — expected to hold, same cophenetic distances.)
- PLACELESS (delocalized). On the recovered mst@0.20 backbone the β trace has **no anatomical address**: nothing clears matched-strength BH at any tested scale (s = 1 / 2.83 / 5.65). Orbitofrontal cortex swings from +91k (p = 0.50) at fine scale to −322k (p = 0.59) at mesoscale — noise, not a hotspot. **This supersedes the whole-graph "β → OFC" result as a degenerate-graph artifact.** OFC survives in this story only as the *encoding* anchor (slide 18), not as the home of the β trace. Localization is the WEAK strength here — we lean on scale-shape (slide 15) and selectivity (slide 14), not on *where*.
- LATERALIZED carriers. The inter-patient spread is not noise: the β trace strength scales with the **left-contact fraction** of the implant — ρ = +0.685, p = 0.029 (✅ mst@0.20). The two resetters are right-hemisphere-only implants. α is a clean control — **not** lateralized (ρ = 0.16, ns). The variability is signal, and the signal is anatomy of coverage, not a home in the brain.

4. SPEECH
So we know β carries the deep, scale-invariant trace. What kind of thing is it? Three answers. First: it's held, not replayed. The β proximity is sustained across the full ten minutes of rest after the task — a cohort-wide reinstatement, ten out of ten patients, one in a thousand — while transient, bursty replay comes back a clean negative. It's a state, not an echo. Second — and here I have to walk something back. Earlier we thought this trace lived in orbitofrontal cortex. On the properly sparsified backbone it doesn't: the β trace is placeless. Nothing clears matched-strength anywhere — fine, meso, coarse — OFC included. It's delocalized. So the "where" is the weak part of this story, and we lean instead on the scale-shape and the selectivity you just saw. Third: the variability across patients is not noise — it's laterality. The strength of the β trace scales with how much of the implant sits in the left hemisphere: correlation of point-six-nine, p equals point-oh-three. The two patients who reset are right-hemisphere-only implants. And alpha is a clean control — no laterality at all. So: held, placeless, and carried by the left implant.

Careful: KILL "β → OFC" — the β trace is DELOCALIZED; OFC belongs to *encoding* (slide 18), not to this slide. Say the OFC walk-back plainly — it's honesty, not weakness. Say "held / sustained / persistent", never "tighter" (tighter is only a trend). Replay = clean negative (transient/burst); the SUSTAINED reinstatement is the positive. Null is matched-strength ONLY — no "drift", no "second null". Read the delocalization PER-SCALE (s = 1 / 2.83 / 5.65), never "best scale". The reinstatement and the tissue/laterality numbers are whole-graph (⚠️) pending mst@0.20 re-derivation — laterality ρ = 0.685 IS re-verified (✅). The "persistent across every traced band" line is the held-not-replayed result generalized (the state characterization), NOT a separate gated cohort test — present it that way if pressed, don't assert a new statistic.
