# C-1 · Take-homes

## Slide placeholder (copy into Canva)

**Main point.** A **multiscale diffusion view** of intracranial connectivity reveals
what single edges and fixed partitions miss: a **held, band-specific hierarchical
trace of inference**, concentrated in **orbitofrontal cortex** — plus a clinical
read-out of epileptogenic tissue for free.

**Concepts to land (the four beats of the talk).**
- **Method.** ImCoh (kills volume conduction) × per-band × **LRG diffusion hierarchy**
  × **matched-strength null**. The key move: **band-specificity *emerges* from the
  multiscale read** — invisible at the raw edge (band-blind), it appears through the
  hierarchy.
- **N1.** A **β cophenetic trace** holds cohort-wide into post-task rest and
  **concentrates in OFC**. Two strength-immune probes (cophenetic + Grassmann)
  **corroborate β** under matched-strength; under the stronger drift null, the
  **cophenetic β** is the robust survivor.
- **N2 (flagship).** The relations the brain **inferred** — not just the pairs it saw
  — **persist offline in β**: an **abstraction of a learned order**, held in rest.
- **N3.** The **same operator** localizes **epileptogenic tissue** (a
  strength-independent diffusion marker) — the cognitive tool doubles as a clinical one.
- **Outlook:** a behavioural link, larger OFC coverage, a prospective clinical test.

## Keep honest (content constraints, not styling)

- **No behavioural data — end on it, plainly.** "Abstraction / consolidation" is an
  interpretation licensed by design + anatomy + literature, **never** a brain–behaviour
  correlation. Don't let the closing line imply one exists.
- **OFC is a hotspot, not a container** (bilateral, ~59 % within-OFC pro, 5/10
  implanted) — *concentration above baseline*, not "the trace lives only in OFC."
- **N1 two-probe claim is matched-strength corroboration; the drift survivor is
  cophenetic β.** Don't overstate it to "both probes survive everything" — Grassmann
  is drift-vulnerable/untested (R-4). Cophenetic β is the dual-null survivor.
- **N2 is fine-grained, not *exclusively* multiscale** — the inference-specific β
  signal is a convergence phenomenon (raw edges / centrality see it too). Keep the
  humility from R-7.
- **Never rank bands by the ×null ratio** — γ_low's is bigger than β's yet it fails
  the gate. The matched-strength Wilcoxon certifies, not the ratio.
- **N3 scope = SOZ labels, not surgical outcome** (R-8). If N3 was cut for time, drop
  its bullet here too.
