# Slide 21 — Take-homes

1. TITLE
Take-homes

2. MAIN CONCEPT
- Method: ImCoh × per-band × LRG diffusion hierarchy × matched-strength — and band-specificity EMERGES from the multiscale read (invisible at the raw edge).
- N1: a β cophenetic trace holds into post-task rest and concentrates in OFC (matched-strength corroboration; cophenetic β is the drift survivor).
- N2 (flagship): the order the brain INFERRED — not just the pairs it saw — persists offline in β. An abstraction of a learned order, held in rest.
- N3: the same operator localizes epileptogenic tissue — the cognitive tool doubles as a clinical one.
- WHY IT MATTERS (the significance beat): this puts all three axes of the multiscale brain into ONE framework — time (the frequency bands), space (the high-resolution sEEG contacts spanning areas), topology (the diffusion hierarchy). That makes it a reusable signature detector: cognition, consolidation, disease may be intrinsically multiscale, and this lens can find such signatures in other data — provided every claim is validated against nulls that discount strength and time.

4. SPEECH
Four things to take home. The method: put imaginary coherence, bands, and the diffusion hierarchy together with a strength-matched null, and band-specificity emerges from the multiscale read — it's invisible at the raw edge. First result: a β trace holds into rest and concentrates in the orbitofrontal cortex. Second, the flagship: it's not just the pairs the brain saw — the order it inferred persists offline, in β. Third: the same operator reads epileptogenic tissue, so the cognitive tool is also clinical. And the deeper why: this framework unifies all three axes of the multiscale brain — time as the frequency bands, space as the sEEG contacts, topology as the diffusion hierarchy — in one lens. That's what makes it more than a result: it's a way to hunt for multiscale signatures — cognition, consolidation, disease — in other data, as long as you always make the nulls discount the boring explanations.

Careful: no behavioural data — "abstraction / consolidation" is licensed by design + anatomy + literature, never a brain–behaviour correlation. OFC is a hotspot, not a container. N1 two-probe claim is matched-strength corroboration; the drift survivor is cophenetic β. N2 is fine-grained, not exclusively multiscale. Never rank bands by the ×null ratio. N3 scope = SOZ labels, not surgical outcome.
