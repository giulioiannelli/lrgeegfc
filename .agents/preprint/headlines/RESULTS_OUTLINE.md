## R1 — the persistence trace
**Title:** *A held, multiscale trace of learning and reasoning.*  
*(alt: What the resting brain keeps after learning and reasoning.)*

- **R1.1 — Reasoning leaves an imprint that rest does not erase.** After the task, the resting network sits measurably closer to the task configuration than it did beforehand, at the cohort level — the reorganization does **not** wash out (in **β**, the clearest case: cohort-median ρ_sym trace +0.20, 6/10 patients above their own strength-matched null, *p* = 0.032; estimator now ρ_sym, arbitrary-half-free — verdict unchanged from ρ_split). It is a **cohort-level** effect over a genuinely variable cohort — the between-patient spread is real and **not** a recording-reliability artifact (detectability control, Methods) — so the claim is never "every patient shows it."
- **R1.2 — The trace consolidates in the orbitofrontal cognitive map.** Where the trace lands is sharply rhythm-specific and biologically pointed. In **β** it concentrates — above each patient's own baseline, bilaterally — in **orbitofrontal cortex**, the hub the brain uses to build relational "cognitive maps," exactly what a transitive-inference task should leave behind (leave-one-out immovable). In **γ_low** a prefrontal concentration appears only within a strong-tracing subgroup, on a band whose cohort trace fails the gate (ρ_sym *p* = 0.080) — and it sits in the very cortex where β is *depleted*, so it does **not** corroborate the map (a demoted aside; prefrontal cortex is a reproducible home in no band). In **α** the trace is real but **scattered**, with no consistent address; the weaker rhythms carry it patient-by-patient (δ, γ_high) or not at all (θ). So **β is the only rhythm that is at once a cohort trace and a reproducible cortical location** — a **hotspot on a distributed trace, not a container** — and β→OFC is the anchor the rest of the paper builds on *(OFC implanted in 5 patients, 4 positive)*.
- **R1.3 — Band-specificity is a property of the network's multiscale geometry.** The band structure is **not** in the raw connectivity: compared edge-by-edge, the cross-phase overlap is positive in **every** band — θ included, indistinguishable from the real ones — and β does not even clear its strength-matched surrogate; a blunt, band-blind drift. Read through the diffusion **hierarchy**, the generic component is filtered out — the non-tracing bands fall to zero, **β** is retained and now clears the surrogate (*p* = 0.032) — so band-specificity **emerges** only once connectivity is read as a multiscale geometry, not a list of edges. A second, **global-mode** readout (Grassmann) of the same operator does **not** merely re-confirm β: the two probes **dissociate by band**, and that is the result — **α** multiscale-only (hierarchy yes, global-mode no), **γ_low** global-only (global-mode yes, hierarchy no), **β** both, **θ** neither. They sort reorganizations **by kind**, not a bar the trace must clear twice. *(Same spectrum → complementary, not independent; full readout × band map in Methods.)*
- **R1.4 — The β trace rides healthy cortex and spares the seizure core.** In **β**, the persistence is carried by coupling between **healthy gray-matter cortex**, and the pairs internal to the seizure core do **not** carry it; removing the seizure-onset contacts altogether leaves the trace intact. So the headline (β) trace is neither a pathology artifact nor an everywhere-and-nowhere effect. *(How the disease core is treated is band-dependent — see R1.6.)*
- **R1.5 — The resting brain dwells in the task's state rather than flashing back to it.** Resolving rest in time, the post-task network does not merely average near the task pattern — it **dwells** there, window after window, at every scale; a hard search for brief, replay-like **bursts** turns up none. So the resting brain does **not** return to where it started: the reorganization is a **held, non-ergodic state**, not a transient or fleeting echo. *(Solid — cohort-wide; the transient-replay reading is a clean negative.)*
- **R1.6 — *(bridge to R3)* Over the seizure core, the memory and inference bands part ways.** β **spares** the epileptic core (R1.4), but the diffuse memory band **α** **recruits** it — there, coupling internal to the seizure core concentrates and clears the strength-matched control. So the cognitive trace and the epileptogenic network are linked, band-dependently — the first bridge to R3.

## R2 — *(flagship)* offline abstraction of a learned structure
**Title:** *Offline abstraction of a learned structure: the trace carries the relations the brain inferred, not just the pairs it saw.*  
*(alt: The brain keeps the relations it reasoned out.)*

- **R2.1 — The brain keeps the relations it reasoned out, not just the pairs it saw.** Splitting the persistent trace into what was **encoded** (premises shown) and what was **inferred** (novel pairs reasoned out, with encoding controlled out), the **inference-specific component itself persists** into post-task rest — in **β alone**. The offline trace carries the *computed* structure, not merely a replay of experience: an offline signature of abstracting a learned order. *(Solid, duration-controlled.)*
- **R2.2 — *(retired 2026-07-09, PI call).*** The τ-sweep "holds across scales" result is dropped: on a fully-connected, continuous-spectrum network there is no meaningful multiscale *across* τ, so the decomposition is read at the single working scale τ=1/λmax, where the dendrogram hierarchy already carries the multiscale content (as in R1.3). The former τ≈2.6 mesoscale-robustness claim is not part of the paper. *(R2.3–R2.7 keep their numbers; the R2.2 slot is retired, mirroring `results_paragraphs/` where `R2.2_mesoscale-integration.tex` is archived.)*
- **R2.3 — The consolidation anchors in the orbitofrontal cognitive map, and learning sets the anchor.** The encoding component concentrates in **orbitofrontal cortex** — the relational-map hub — and the pure memorising phase, with no reasoning demanded, already leaves its own α/β trace there, so OFC is anchored by **both** task phases. *(Solid.)*
- **R2.4 — The two components live in different rhythms.** The **memory** component persists in **both α and β**; the **inference-specific** component persists in **β alone**. Not an artifact of the longer test session — the β inference component does **not** scale with recording length (ρ_sym Spearman ρ=+0.10, p=0.78); length-tracking sits in **non-tracing** bands (α ρ=+0.55, high-γ ρ=+0.70), disjoint from the inference band. *(Solid, duration-controlled. NB: earlier "only α tracks length" corrected 2026-07-07 — high-γ tracks harder, but both are inference-null.)*
- **R2.5 — A hidden memory trace surfaces in the cingulate at low-γ.** A focal, encoding-related trace appears in the **cingulate in low-γ** that is completely invisible at the whole-brain level — the clearest case of the method reading a single region. *(Solid.)*
- **R2.6 — Inference points toward the cingulate.** The inference-specific β component's only cortical concentration is **cingulate** — clearing the full-length strength null (ρ_sym BH q=0.030 incl / 0.040 excl), away from the OFC encoding anchor — but it does **not** survive length-matching (q=0.15/0.30), so the concentration is length-assisted. *(Directional lead — consistent sign, length-assisted; NOT an established location. audit_160/161.)*
- **R2.7 — The cingulate seems to switch its content by rhythm.** Read together, the cingulate carries **memory at low-γ and inference at β** — one region multiplexing different cognitive content across frequencies. *(Suggestive — the inference half is a hint.)*

## R3 — the same organization identifies epileptogenic tissue
**Title:** *Propagator-inspired markers of epileptogenic tissue.*  
*(alt: Epileptogenic tissue read from the network propagator.)*

- **R3.1 — Seizure contacts form a co-organized network group — they are *not* identifiable one contact at a time.** A node-by-node "is this contact epileptic?" label does not transfer across patients; read **relationally**, the seizure contacts form a **strength-independent** group that co-diffuses (**δ strongest, multiband; AUC 0.80**). Because the |ImCoh| substrate is volume-conduction-immune this is not spatial proximity — and given a few as seeds, the group points to the others, including **distant** contacts on entirely separate electrodes (δ, off-shaft AUC 0.72).
- **R3.2 — Fused across rhythms, it becomes a calibrated seizure-zone probability — carried by δ.** The single strongest band (**δ**) is turned into a **calibrated, cross-patient probability** of seizure onset; fusing all six bands leaves discrimination essentially unchanged (AUC 0.76→0.81) but **sharpens top-of-list precision** (34%→60%), the triage-relevant gain.
- **R3.3 — Patients fall into two regimes.** Detection behaves one way when the seizure zone forms a tight community and differently when it sits as a **network hub** — two populations the method must treat separately.
- **R3.4 — Honest scope.** Everything here is validated against **clinical seizure-onset labels, not surgical outcome** — a triage/shortlist tool to focus clinical attention, not a standalone localizer.
