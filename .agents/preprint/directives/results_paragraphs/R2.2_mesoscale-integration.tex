\paragraph{The inference-specific component holds across the hierarchy's scales.}
The persistent \(\beta\) component was read above at the hierarchy's finest scale, set by the smallest diffusion time \(\tau\); larger \(\tau\) coarsens the same operator from single-edge splits toward network communities. Whether it is tied to that finest scale or is multiscale is a pointed question, because transitive inference is not pairwise but a chaining of premises --- A\,\(>\)\,B, B\,\(>\)\,C, C\,\(>\)\,D \(\Rightarrow\) A\,\(>\)\,D --- and larger \(\tau\) integrates exactly such multi-step paths, weighting longer walks over single edges; if the component reflects the inferred order it should survive coarsening, not sit at the finest scale alone. How far \(\tau\) can rise is set by the operator: with \(Z_{\mathrm{eff}}\) the number of diffusion modes the propagator retains (of \(N \approx 118\) contacts), \(Z_{\mathrm{eff}} \approx 72\) at the fine scale and \(\approx 35\) at \(\tau \approx 2.6/\lambda_{\max}\), but only \(\approx 6\) by \(\tau \approx 7/\lambda_{\max}\) --- a near-uniform propagator with no hierarchy left to read. Re-tested against the same strength-matched surrogate at the coarsest still-resolved scale, \(\tau \approx 2.6/\lambda_{\max}\), the \(\beta\) component clears it just as at the fine scale: cohort-median inference-specific persistence \({+}0.13\) (surrogate \({+}0.01\), \(p = 0.005\), \gls{loo} \(p = 0.010\), \(7/10\) patients), and \({+}0.09\) at the fine scale (surrogate \({+}0.01\), \(p = 0.010\), \(6/10\)) --- with no other band clearing at either scale (strongest control \(p \approx 0.25\)). A coarse-graining artifact --- coarsening compresses any strength-heterogeneous hierarchy --- is ruled out by the surrogate itself: coarsened by the identical operation, it would show the same generic compression, yet its median holds at \({+}0.01\), leaving the \(\beta\) value as a real margin above the null. The \(\beta\) trace is thus multiscale --- carried at the coarse scale of multi-step paths as well as the finest splits, \(\beta\)-specific at each --- present where relational chaining would register, not confined to one scale.
