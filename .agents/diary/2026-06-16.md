# 2026-06-16 — distant-SOZ marker: from verdict to deployable tool

**Head.** Built the figure + plain-language walkthrough for the verified δ heat-kernel
distant-SOZ marker, then pushed it toward deployment and kept it honest: the precision is a
**good ranker but a concentrated shortlist** (not a clean catch); the propagator+strength
**compound improves via a hard seed-regime SWITCH — never a blend** (the blend secretly
damages the strong responders, and the cohort median hid it); and a **P(SOZ) is emittable,
calibrated at the top but honestly modest** (~0.1–0.35). No verdict flips; the core δ marker
(AUC 0.72, 8/10, off-shaft, null p=0) stands and now has a deployment story around it.

## The numbers (all off-shaft; CSVs under data/audit/epi_marker_{precision,compound}/)

- **Precision (audit_103):** off-shaft SOZ prevalence ~4%; cohort-median **lift@5 ≈ 2.2×
  (responders), recall@10 ≈ 0.34**. Strongly **bimodal** — Pat_08/14/05 get 9–15× lift, others
  ~0 at top-5 even with decent AUC (precision@k with tiny targets-per-shaft is brutal). Good
  ranker, concentrated shortlist. Candidate lists are anatomically coherent (Pat_13 distal
  hippocampus, Pat_08 superior-temporal + frontal operculum, Pat_02 lateral OFC) but are
  **hypotheses with no ground truth**.
- **Compound (audit_104):** **switch, don't blend.** Hard seed-regime gate (strength if mean
  seed strength-percentile r ≥ 0.70, else pure propagator) → AUC **0.716 → 0.750, 9/10**,
  *preserving* responders (Pat_08 stays 0.95) and rescuing Pat_15 (0.21 → 0.93). The continuous
  blend raised cohort lift but **damaged the best responders** (Pat_08 0.95 → 0.56) — the
  cohort median hid it. Regime detector imperfect: Pat_10 unrecoverable, borderline r≈0.72
  ambiguous.
- **P(SOZ) (audit_104):** LOPO logistic on [affinity, strength] — calibrated at the top
  (P≈0.13 → 13% observed), held-out AUC median 0.79 for responders (not hubs), but values
  **modest** (top ~0.1–0.35) and Brier ≈ the 4% prevalence floor. A triage score, not a
  per-node verdict.

## Lesson (persisted)

**A cohort-median AUC/lift can lie** — the blend's higher cohort lift was bought by damaging
individual responders. Always check per-patient before crediting a compound or any aggregate.
