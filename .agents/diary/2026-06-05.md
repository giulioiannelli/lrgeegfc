## Session 2 — `audit_80` per-node marker classifier (Direction 2 complete)

**Context.** User lifted the gate: build the classifier — validate by masking
known epi contacts then discover new candidates not yet labelled.

**Built.** `scripts/01_compute/audit/audit_80_epi_marker_classifier.py` (reads
the audit_79 feature cache, no recompute) + figure
`scripts/02_preprint/preprint_33_epi_marker_verdict.py`. Three protocols:
(1) leave-one-patient-out (LOPO) cross-patient discrimination, class-balanced
logistic, PR-AUC headline (prevalence ≈9.5%); (2) within-patient label-
permutation null R=200 (exact for the label-free trace/strength/geometry sets);
(3) **whole-shaft masking** — blank a whole epi-bearing shaft, recompute the
label-dependent probe features from visible labels only, predict the masked
shaft → kills the same-shaft-neighbour tautology by construction. Probe features
split into `probe_label` (leaky tautology) vs `probe_geom` (label-free electrode
geometry). Discovery = label-free `trace_strength` scores every non-epi node;
candidate list + cross-band agreement + 3D distance (mm) to nearest known epi.

**Verdict — NEGATIVE, geometry_dominant every band (brutally honest).**
- **No trace-based marker.** LOPO trace PR-AUC at prevalence (best β 0.124 vs
  0.095, perm-p 0.080 — not significant); dAUC2 ≤ 0 (adds nothing to baseline).
- **Node strength does NOT transfer cross-patient** (PR ≤ prevalence, perm-p
  ≈ 1.0). The within-patient "epi hyperconnected" AUCs from audit_79 (δ 0.76 /
  β 0.65) are patient-relative FC-scale, not a cross-patient threshold — the key

## Session 4 — `audit_82` WITHIN-patient propagator-subspace recovery (POSITIVE)

**Context.** User (rightly) pushed back: the cross-patient classifiers were the
wrong framing; "rediscover an unlabelled epi node" is a within-patient
anomaly/template problem in a rich subspace built from the propagator ρ(τ) /
information diffusion. I had not tried that.

**Built.** `scripts/01_compute/audit/audit_82_epi_propagator_recovery.py` + scope
§11 + figure `scripts/02_preprint/preprint_34_epi_recovery.py`. Feature subspace:
LRG propagator ρ(τ)=e^{−τL}/Z (return prob ρ_ii(τ), diffusion-row entropy,
communication distance T_ρ=1/ρ) from cached eigh(L_post) over a per-patient
τ-grid, + per-pair trace (audit_79) + leverage (audit_81). Standardise WITHIN
patient (scale-free). Leave-one-epi-out: template = mean of the other epi,
recover the held-out node by similarity; recovery AUC = P(held-out epi > non-epi).
Controls: feature-set ablation (strength/diffusion/trace/full), off-shaft
negatives (geometry), random-set permutation null.

**Result — POSITIVE.** Within-patient recovery works: full AUC β 0.715 / θ 0.679
/ low-γ 0.637 / α 0.629 (per patient up to 0.85, Pat_07/14). **The propagator
ρ(τ) subspace ADDS beyond node strength in 5/6 bands** (cross-band median full
0.633 vs strength 0.599); **at β diffusion is the PRIMARY driver (0.720 vs
strength 0.622)**. δ exception = pure hubness. Three controls pass: off-shaft ≈
all-negative (β 0.730/0.715 → not shaft proximity); beats the random-set perm
null in 6/9 patients at β/θ; and **Pat_08 is the strength-orthogonality proof —
its epi are not hubs (strength recovery 0.165) yet the diffusion subspace
recovers them at 0.758**. Discovery (`propagator_candidates.csv`): 7 contacts
top-ranked in ≥4 bands, 2 of them far (>20 mm) from any known epi (Pat_06 U9 @41
mm, Pat_13 W'1 @26 mm) = the genuinely novel flags; the rest likely field
continuation.

**Reconciliation with audit_80/81.** Not a contradiction: those tested
cross-patient supervised transfer (a global threshold), which fails on
patient-specific FC scale. audit_82 is within-patient, self-normalised — the
scale confound disappears, and the propagator carries real strength-orthogonal
epi structure. **The honest verdict is now: a propagator-subspace epi marker
EXISTS within-patient (the partial-label "find the rest" scenario); it does NOT
exist as a cross-patient universal threshold.** Bounds: AUC ~0.6–0.72 =
useful-not-clinical; heterogeneous across patients.

## Session 6 — Directions 1 & 2 (hide-and-seek + signed magnetic Laplacian) → DEPTH confound, both negative

**Context.** User: validate the α propagator story by hide-and-seek (mask known
epi, recover them) to ensure it holds beyond hubness; and pursue a NEW direction —
signed ImCoh + eigenmodes of the signed Laplacian.

**Built.** `audit_87` (hide-50%-of-epi Fisher recovery + within-shaft control),
`audit_88` (signed-ImCoh Hermitian magnetic Laplacian `L_H = D̄ − iA` — signed
ImCoh is ANTISYMMETRIC, so the Kunegis `D̄−A` is non-Hermitian; the magnetic
Laplacian is the correct operator) + scope `2026-06-05_signed-magnetic-laplacian-epi.md`.

**Arc (and the third retraction).** audit_87 within-shaft: MS-z propagator
recovers hidden epi >chance all 6 bands (p≤0.02), strength collapses within-shaft
at δ/high-γ — looked like a real shaft-independent marker. audit_88: directional
features also recover within-shaft all bands (δ 0.717). THEN the control I'd
missed: **along-shaft DEPTH (`contact_index_norm`)** recovers hidden epi
within-shaft at 0.72–0.74 every band, BEATS both propagator (0.60–0.67) and
directional (0.60–0.71), and **dynamics add nothing to depth (paired p≥0.33).**
So both "positives" were the depth confound — epileptic contacts sit at
characteristic electrode depths (geometry/known anatomy), and our dynamics
machinery only partially re-encodes depth.

**FINAL VERDICT (epi-contact marker).** No dynamics-based marker. Within-patient
recoverability = node strength (hubness, gross) + along-shaft depth (fine), both
geometric. Neither the LRG propagator subspace nor the signed-ImCoh magnetic
Laplacian adds beyond these. Cross-patient null (audit_80/81). Three retractions
this session, each caught by the next control: (1) β breakthrough = unfair
template-distance strength baseline; (2) audit_86 "modest α" MS-z; (3) within-shaft
recovery = depth. The magnetic-Laplacian operator is correct and kept for future
directional-FC questions; Stage-2 sign surrogate NOT warranted.

**Lesson (now in memory).** ALWAYS add an along-shaft depth (contact position)
baseline to any within-shaft epi-contact test — it is the dominant fine-scale
confound and it masquerades as a dynamics signal.
