# 2026-04-15

## Session — the ImCoh reset

backfilled: 2026-04-24

bug in `compute_lrg_analysis` — magnitude was being applied after
band-averaging when Jensen's inequality requires magnitude first. LRG
output for the squared-ImCoh cache was silently wrong.

**Done:** Diagnosed the bug; switched the canonical FC to
`<|ImCoh|>_f` (magnitude-first, then band-average); wrote full reset
report (`IMCOH_RESET_REPORT.md`, 539 lines). Marked the five days of
|ImCoh|² outputs as QUANTITATIVE_STALE. Produced companion docs:
process report, verification results, Pat_02 + controls, gap analysis,
Section 2 figures handoff.

**Issues:** Every numerical claim in |ImCoh|²-era reports invalidated.
Figures and H2 statistics need reruns under the new cache.

**Decisions:**
- `imcoh_abs = <|ImCoh|>_f` is canonical going forward (current era).
- `imcoh_sq` kept as a loader option for backcompat only.
- Pre-reset reports retained but flagged STALE; do not cite their
  numbers.

**Next:** Port H1–H4 to IMCOH_ABS; re-run the epileptic-zone analysis
under the corrected FC.
