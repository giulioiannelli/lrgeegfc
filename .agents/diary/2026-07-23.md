**Head.** Continues the per-patient-differences exploration opened 2026-07-14 (starting point + 5-point critical preamble in `.agents/diary/2026-07-14.md`). **Context.** Cached-only, zero FC/LRG recompute. Sources: `data/sparsified_arc/ms_mst020/per_patient_scale.csv` (10 pat × 6 band × 16 scale `obs_rho` + matched-strength null `surr_p50/p95`, `p`, `N`) joined to `data/audit/implant_geometry/per_patient_features.csv` (coverage: hemisphere / lobe / `sigma_disp`, plus SOZ `frac_epi`). **Done / findings (mesoscale s=5.65, n=10):**
- Fluctuation ≠ one thing. Cross-patient IQR ranks low_γ 0.49 ≫ high_γ 0.30 > β 0.23 ≈ δ 0.22 ≈ α 0.20 ≫ θ 0.05 — but the two most "fluctuating" (low_γ / high_γ) have ~0 cohort effect (med_rho .034 / .048, negative margins). Their spread is noise scattered around zero, not an unstable finding: there is no finding to be unstable.
- β spread is real + benign: all 10 patients ≥ 0, and obs_rho tracks left-hemisphere coverage (`B_hemi` ρ+0.68*, `left_frac` +0.59) → reproduces audit_148 (β heterogeneity = laterality). Coverage (source #3), not a latent patient trait.
- α is the tightest real band (IQR 0.198), positive median, one anti patient → near-threshold, not wild scatter.
- Methodological caveat surfaced: per-patient `obs_rho` co-varies with its own MS null height (`surr_p50`) — β ρ+0.83*, high_γ +0.79*, δ +0.70*, low_γ +0.68*. Part of the cross-patient obs_rho spread is spread in baseline non-identifiability. Use the margin (obs_rho − null), not raw obs_rho.
- SOZ null: `frac_epi` explains NO per-patient trace variation in ANY band (all ns; δ −0.02, β +0.27, α −0.30). → the 2026-07-14 pathology idea gets no support at the trace level.
- Between-band (6 pts, weak/ecological): IQR ~ SNR-gating rank-biserial ρ+0.94 p=.00; IQR ~ null-height ρ+0.26 ns. Consistent with source #1 (SNR) setting which bands are noisy, but n=6.

**Issues / caveats of this cut (tighten before hardening):** single mesoscale slice (bands peak at different scales; per-patient scale_range ≈ 0.2–0.27 for all); n=10 Spearman corrs fragile (a single * ≈ ρ 0.65); the `loo_swing` metric was degenerate (constant = 1, uninformative) — needs a real LOO-of-cohort-verdict; L3 is 6-band ecological.
