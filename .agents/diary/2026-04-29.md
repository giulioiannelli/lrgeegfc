## Session 5 — Critical post-mortem + pivot to eigenvector-direct probes (E-series)

**Context:** User pushed back on the v1 verdict ("delta only with task
trace looks delusional") and on the triangulation reliability ("are
you sure these are reliable checkers for our core message?"). After
honest examination, decided to pause the dendrogram-based ladder and

adapt to eigenvector-based rather than scalar-eigenvalue-based; the
ρ̂(τ) and D matrices probe diffusion patterns through edge
heterogeneity, but S(τ) and C(τ) are trivial scalars; we need the
spectral probe to identify diffusion patterns directly.

**Done:**
- **Critical post-mortem report** at
  `.agents/reports/2026-04-29_critical-post-mortem.md`. Inventories
  every measure tried since the imcoh_abs reset (13 distinct measures
  across 5 families), scores each, identifies 5 things we know with
  confidence (δ partition ridge real; strict-J cohort null; H2c-shared
  artefactual; spectrum-scalars dead; infrastructure works), 5 things
  we thought we knew but don't (cohort-wide multi-band trace claim;
  triangulation-strengthens-verdict; dendrogram preserves spatial
  info; per-leaf cohort census; pre-reg = sensitivity), 3 open
  questions (cognitive vs confound; eigenvector-direct visible? cohort
  framing under implant heterogeneity), 5 methodological lessons.
- **Eigenvector-direct pivot plan** at
  `.agents/plans/active/2026-04-29_eigenvector-direct-pivot-plan.md`.
  Defines three new rungs:
  - **E1 — Spectral subspace alignment.** Top-k eigenvectors of L̂ as
    Grassmann-manifold subspace; chordal distance via principal angles
    (singular values of `V_a^T V_b`). Trace direction: subspace(test)
    closer to subspace(post) than subspace(pre) is. Half-baseline null
    from halves cache. k ∈ {2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21}, ridge ≥ 2.
  - **E2 — Diffusion-map embedding drift.** Coifman-Lafon embedding
    Ψ(x) = (exp(-τλ_i/2) v_i(x)) for top-k eigenvectors. Per-node
    embedding-distance across phases. Critical caveat: eigenvector
    sign+ordering ambiguity → must align via Procrustes-on-spectral-basis
    (`align_eigenvectors` library helper to be promoted).
  - **E3 — IPR shifts.** Per-eigenvector inverse participation ratio
    (Σ v(x)^4); Wasserstein-1 distance between IPR distributions across
    phases. Sign-invariant (4th power) so signs don't matter; Wasserstein
    on distribution sidesteps ordering issue.
- **Critical methodological distinction from the dead residual-subspace
  probe:** that probe used rank-1 leading eigenvectors of *residuals*
  (D^task − D^pre vs D^post − D^pre); E-series uses full rank-k
