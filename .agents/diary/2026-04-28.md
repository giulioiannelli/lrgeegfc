# 2026-04-28 (session 2) — functional tree distance, τ-curve framework, primitive 4-patient grid

## Head

Reframed the phase-pair comparison from the start, dropping cut-based
partition similarity (`k` is arbitrary) and the single-τ cophenetic
projection. The principled object is a τ-indexed family of matrices per
phase; phase-pair similarity is the per-τ correlation curve
`δ(τ; A, B) = 1 − corr(triu(M_A(τ)), triu(M_B(τ)))`, evaluated as snapshot
at `τ = max(1/λ_max^A, 1/λ_max^B)` and as `d(log τ)`-integral over
`I = [τ_min, τ\* = argmax C(τ)]`. Two parallel views run in lock-step:
`δ_S` = Spearman on `D = 1/ρ` (dendrogram topology, robust to `1/ρ`
divergences) and `δ_P` = Pearson on `ρ` (height-aware, well-behaved on the
bounded similarity). On the primitive 4-patient grid (Pat_02/03/06/13 ×
6 bands), Pat_03 and Pat_06 show task-trace persistence
(`Δ(test, post) < Δ(pre, post)`) at every band, Pat_02 at 4/6, Pat_13 at
1/6 — heterogeneous, not a clean cohort verdict but a clean *visual*.

## What the data shows on the primitive set

Cohort-of-4 medians of `Δ_S` (rank, topology) per band:

```
band         (pre,learn)  (pre,test)  (pre,post)  (learn,test)  (learn,post)  (test,post)
delta            0.568       0.538       0.509         0.206         0.359         0.285
theta            0.557       0.522       0.434         0.167         0.448         0.440
alpha            0.510       0.475       0.418         0.231         0.376         0.325
beta             0.658       0.524       0.533         0.359         0.575         0.416
low_gamma        0.696       0.627       0.657         0.336         0.637         0.519
high_gamma       0.570       0.433       0.574         0.375         0.486         0.338
```

Reading rules:

1. **(learn, test) is always smallest** — the two task phases form the
   tightest cluster at every band (Δ_S ≈ 0.17–0.38). Expected; sanity check
   passes.
2. **Task-trace persistence ⇔ `Δ(test, post) < Δ(pre, post)`** holds at the
   median in **5/6 bands**: delta, alpha, beta, low_gamma, high_gamma —
   theta is tied. Same direction in `Δ_P`.
3. **Per-patient task-trace contrast `Δ(test,post) − Δ(pre,post)` < 0:**
   - Pat_02: 4/6 bands (delta, beta, low_gamma, high_gamma)
   - Pat_03: 6/6 bands
   - Pat_06: 6/6 bands
   - Pat_13: 1/6 bands (high_gamma only) — anti-trace dissenter
4. **Topology vs height co-vary** — `δ_S` and `δ_P` track each other within
   ~0.05 in nearly every cell; the rare divergences are at low_gamma where
   `δ_P` < `δ_S` (kernel rescaling without rank flip).

## What this is, what this is not

- **IS:** a primitive-set, no-scalar-gate, no-`k`-axis, τ-physical
  visualisation of phase-pair similarity. Both topology and height views
  in lock-step. Heterogeneity at the patient level visible — 1 of 4
  patients is anti-trace.
- **IS NOT:** a cohort verdict (only n=4 here), a hypothesis test, or a
  per-leaf / per-subtree localisation. The continuous-trace matrix and
  Cohesion-CBR scopes remain the right tools for those questions.
- **Open follow-ups (per scope §9):** smoothing-window sensitivity for
  `τ\*`, common-giant-component drop-rate audit, probe-bias control,
  `n=10` cohort generalisation, `log ρ` Pearson sanity check.

## Reconciliation with this morning's residual-subspace verdict

This morning's finding: residual `D^task − D^pre` and `D^post − D^pre` do
not align above phase-label-permutation null. The functional-tree-distance
result above is *not* in tension with that — it measures a different
thing. Residual subspace asks "is the *direction* of change shared?";
functional-tree asks "is the post tree closer to the test tree than to
the pre tree on the τ-axis?". These can diverge (as they do for Pat_13)
or agree (Pat_03/06). The complementarity is the point — neither view
alone is a cohort verdict.

# 2026-04-28 (session 3) — within-baseline split-half null collapses the FTD task-trace claim

## Head

Implemented and ran the within-baseline split-half null for the
functional-tree-distance contrast on the same primitive 4-patient set.
For each (patient, band, phase), the timeseries was split at T//2,
|ImCoh| FC computed per half via the existing
`compute_imcoh_abs_halves`, and the FTD machinery (Δ_S = rank, Δ_P =
height) evaluated on the half-Laplacians at the full-data anchor τ-grid.
The within-phase Δ across 4 phases is the noise floor; the contrast
`C = Δ(test, post) − Δ(pre, post)` is signal only when `|C| > within_med`.
**Verdict on rank view (Δ_S): only Pat_06 has above-noise task-trace,
and only at 3/6 bands (δ, θ, γ_h). Pat_02, Pat_03, Pat_13 all 0/6.**
The height view (Δ_P) is more permissive — Pat_06 lights up 6/6, but
Pat_02 and Pat_03 only manage 1-2/6. The strong-looking heatmap from
session 2 was almost entirely *within-phase variability*, not real
task-induced reorganisation. This is the within-baseline gate the
session-1 residual-subspace finding had already foreshadowed — same
direction of failure, different geometric primitive.

## Where I spent the day mistakenly

The framework is mathematically the right object — Villegas 2025 Eq. 1
communication distance evaluated on a τ-grid, with rank vs height as
two genuine projections, with split-half within-phase null as the gate.
The choices are defensible. **What's wrong is the same thing that's
been wrong all month: the cohort task-trace claim simply isn't there
at the geometric whole-tree level under our continuous-spectrum,
fully-connected, weight-heterogeneous FC graphs.**

The session-2 obs heatmap on n=4 (Pat_02/03/06/13) looked like a
cohort signal because (a) the n=4 set was Pat_03/06-biased and (b) no
noise gate was applied. Once we expand to n=10 and gate by within-phase
median, the "5/6 bands trace" cohort-median direction collapses. This
is the third reading of the same pattern — see also the
post-mortem-scalar-session of April.

## What this means for tomorrow

Pivot decision is unchanged from this morning's verdicts:

1. **Stop adding measures** for the cohort task-trace question.
   Eight measures down. The ninth has the same prior as the eighth.
2. **Commit to H2c α/β under continuous controls** as the cohort
   deliverable. H2c measures *directional alignment* of pair-wise
   distance shifts (`Spearman(D^test − D^pre, D^post − D^pre)` per
   patient), not closeness ranking of trees themselves; it survived
   continuous controls at n=10 (p=0.008/0.014, 8/10 patients each).
   Different geometric statement than FTD; only one survives.
3. **Pat_06 as a per-patient case study**, Pat_15 as a per-patient
   dissenter — both for the writeup as illustrations of *the
   spread*, not as cohort claims.
4. **FTD framework remains as a per-patient diagnostic** — it cleanly
   visualises which cells are in noise vs which clear the floor.
   Useful for the H2c writeup as a geometric companion. Not a cohort
   gate.
