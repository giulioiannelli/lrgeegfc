# 2026-08-31 — W0-B null ladder closed

## Head

The α/β cross-phase trace is real and tied to the task, but **its content is not lag-specific**, and that is the result that constrains what the paper may claim. It clears every independence null (matched-strength β 16/16, α 12/16; the independent segment-lattice N1b β 16/16, α 13/16, agreeing with matched-strength at r = 0.983) and it clears session repartitioning for β (exact rotation test, 10/16 scales, best p = 0.0049, 8/16 LOO-robust). But every lag-destroying null that preserves coherence magnitude fails at every band and scale, and the fixed-backbone control — built to give the trace its best chance by holding the graph identical — shows **no separation at all**: cohort median margins −0.003 (β) and +0.004 (α) against +0.214 and +0.130 under matched-strength.

## What changed

- **New library module** `utils/surrogate/timeseries_nulls.py`: surrogates acting on per-segment Fourier coefficients or the complex band coherency, i.e. upstream of the adjacency matched-strength shuffles. Five families with literature anchors (Theiler 1992; Perkel, Gerstein & Moore 1967).
- **`heat_multiscale` gains** `partial_spearman`, `cross_phase_functionals`, `cross_phase_functionals_over_scales` — promoted from a script-local copy in `sparsified_arc/05_enc_inf_arc.py` on its second caller, with an explicit warning that a partial correlation is not zero-centred under no-signal input.
- **Ten runners** under `scripts/01_compute/null_ladder/`, all artifacts under `data/paper_final/w0b_nulls/`.

## Findings worth carrying forward

1. **The brief's premise needed correcting.** A circular shift is a pure phase rotation only in the single-segment DFT sense. Under the Welch estimator the pipeline actually uses, a whole-recording roll destroys coherence *magnitude* (mean |C| 0.241 → 0.036) — it is an independence null, not a lag null. N1 (frequency domain, magnitude-preserving) and N1b (time domain) are therefore separate rungs, not variants.
2. **Matched-strength has been acting as an independence null.** It agrees with the mechanically unrelated N1b at r = 0.983 across 800 cells. Good news for the β result's robustness; also the reason matched-strength never reached the magnitude-versus-lag question.
3. **No cross-phase functional is zero-centred.** All four — `T_test` included, not just the conditional — return significantly positive values at 16/16 scales for α and β on a five-phase sham carved from one resting recording *with temporal order destroyed*. Ordered sham ≈ 2× shuffled sham, so drift contributes but is not the whole story; the construction itself manufactures positives. Fed independent white noise the functionals return −0.031..+0.030, so it is not the algebra. **This closes the placebo gap W0-C left open on `T_infspec_pe`.**
4. **The whole encoding/inference decomposition fails the lag null**: across 320 (functional × band × scale) tests, min q = 0.801.
5. **N1's null is concretely "the pipeline on ordinary coherence"** (r = 0.879), and `⟨|C|⟩` yields a *larger* median ρ_sym than `|ImCoh|` in both trace bands on 5 patients. Handed to W0-A as a defined experiment, with the trade stated: `⟨|C|⟩` is not volume-conduction-immune.
6. **Cleared one candidate artifact**: the A/B `nperseg` asymmetry (halves at `nperseg//2`, task/post at full) is *not* material — median shift −0.056..+0.065, 1/32 cells nominally significant, sign not even consistent. The incumbent A/B construction does not need respecifying.

## Inheritance rule for the other lanes

Matched-strength alone is no longer sufficient. Every cohort claim needs three rungs: matched-strength or N1b (is there coupling structure at all), **N1 with the backbone fixed** (is it carried by lag), and **N3 order-preserving** (is it tied to the task or the session). Where N1 is not cleared — currently α and β at every scale, and all four functionals — the claim must be stated as being about **coherence structure**, not lagged interaction, and volume-conduction immunity must not be offered as its justification. And no cross-phase functional may ever be tested against zero.
