# 2026-06-22

## Duration confound on the inference arc RESOLVED — β duration-robust; naive truncation null retired as invalid

**Head.** Closed the open gate on N2 (encoding-vs-inference headline): is the
whole-brain β inference-specific consolidation arc a duration artifact (`task_test`
is 1.35–2.53× longer than `task_learn`, and `f = D_test − D_learn` inherits it)?
**Verdict: β is duration-ROBUST.** But the instrument I built to test it — the
length-matched strength null rebuilt on a *truncated* `task_test`
(`audit_103b`, the "stage-2-arc") — turned out **INVALID**, and the verdict had to
be carried by cleaner controls. N2 cascaded + committed; stays unified (count 4).

## What happened (the honest arc of the session)

1. **Built `audit_103b`** (whole-brain truncation null, max reuse of audit_103 +
   audit_113b machinery; gates: encoding bit-identical 1e-17, β surrogate reuses
   audit_113b's exact cached ensemble). β at matched length: **p=0.0068 — identical
   to full-length.** Looked like a clean pass.
2. **Ran all 6 bands.** Surprise: **α also "clears"** at matched length (0.080 →
   0.0029), breaking the clean β-vs-α dissociation. Flagged it as a possible
   retraction.
3. **Negative-control check (the save).** Ran the genuinely-null bands and a
   placement sweep. **δ — dead-null at full length (p=0.54) — false-positives at
   p≈0.014 at BOTH head and center windows** (and at center out-clears β). The
   matched-length surrogate median sits at ~−0.03 in *every* band: a contiguous
   truncation injects a common-mode structural shift into `D_test` that the
   *un-demeaned* whole-brain concordance reads as fake signal (worst in slow bands).
   ⇒ **the truncation arc null is invalid** and the "α clears" surprise is the same
   artifact, not a real α effect. (This is *why* the demeaned localization control
   `audit_113b` stayed clean — demeaning removes the common mode.)
4. **Clean replacement control.** Per-patient regression of full-length
   `T_infspec_pe` vs the test/learn length ratio (artifact-free): **β does not scale
   (ρ≈+0.25, p≈0.49); α carries the duration-tracking trend (ρ≈+0.53).** Combined
   with the mandatory full-length matched-strength referee (β p≈0.007), this
   establishes **β inference arc = duration-robust; α = duration-suspect** → β-only
   is the *duration-clean* verdict (α is exactly the band the confound would
   inflate, and it's the suspect one).

## Lesson

**Always run a negative-control band through a new truncation/subsampling null
before trusting it.** β "passing" looked decisive until δ (a known-null band) passed
just as hard — the test was manufacturing significance, not measuring it. The clean
across-subject regression sidestepped the artifact entirely. Also: un-demeaned
whole-brain concordance is corruptible by contiguous-window truncation; the
per-system demeaned read-out is not (worth a methods sentence).
